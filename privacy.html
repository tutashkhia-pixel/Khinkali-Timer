Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Khinkali Timer, a productivity tool built by TechEdge Labs LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Khinkali Timer operates primarily on your device. We do not use remote databases to store your personal tasks, timer history, or personal identifiers. We use local browser storage (localStorage/IndexedDB) to save your settings.

Language detection

On your first visit, the app asks Cloudflare's public trace service which country you are visiting from, only to choose English or Georgian. We do not store or send this anywhere else, and you can change the language at any time.

Khinkali Focus Blocker (Chrome extension)

If you install the optional extension, it checks the address of the page you open only while your focus timer is running, and sends you back to the timer if that page is on its short list of distracting sites. Its settings stay in your browser. It does not record or send your browsing history.

Story images

When you create a share image of your focus session, it is made inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it yourself.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use standard web storage technologies to save your local preferences, such as your selected language, tasks and focus history. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache.
